Labs

These pages are experiments with the same data, and the main map remains the map of record.

  • Lamps

    Lights show the distribution of places of worship on a globe under the sun for the moment you choose. Zoom in to see a pillar for each mapped place.

  • Relief: density and diversity in three dimensions

    One city at a time, divided into hexagonal cells. Each cell's column rises with the number of places of worship mapped in it and is banded by tradition, and every place in the study area is counted once.

Both maps use places tagged as places of worship in OpenStreetMap as of 1 September 2026. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Project compilation CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
