Data maps

Places of worship with census and religion-data overlays, by country.

Albania 2 waves · 2011–2023
Prefecture census religion, 2011–2023, from INSTAT; non-response is historically large, so shares use a stated-response denominator that excludes it.
Australia 2 waves · 2016–2021
Statistical-area-2 census religious affiliation, 2016–2021; the 2011 wave awaits an official boundary correspondence.
Austria 7 waves · 1951–2021
Bundesland census religious-affiliation overlays, 1951–2021; the 2021 wave is a mixed register-plus-survey estimate, not a census count.
Bahamas 1 wave · 2010
Island census religion, 2010, from the BNSI island reports; the 2022 census reports religion nationally only, so it is not mapped by island.
Brazil 3 waves · 2000–2022
Municipality and state census religion overlays, 2000–2022.
Bulgaria 3 waves · 2001–2021
District census religion (National Statistical Institute, NSI), 2001–2021; a voluntary question whose published basis differs in every wave (full population, then respondents, then excluding additions without religion information), and the waves are separate snapshots with no change metric.
Burkina Faso 2 waves · 2006–2019
Regional census religious affiliation (INSD), 2006–2019; 2006 is exact counts on the full resident population and 2019 is published one-decimal shares on the collected-person basis, and no change metric crosses the two bases. INSD publication reuse rights are unresolved.
Canada 3 waves · 2001–2021
Census-division religion, 2001–2021, each wave on its own boundary vintage (2011 is the voluntary NHS).
Côte d'Ivoire 1 wave · 2021
Sub-prefecture and commune census religious affiliation (INS/ANStat RGPH 2021), on an ordinary-household denominator; the source's own printed arithmetic differs by one to three people across many rows and ships unchanged with disclosure, and the Bona National Park boundary unit carries no census data. 2021 only, no change metric.
Croatia 3 waves · 2001–2021
County census religious-affiliation overlays (Croatian Bureau of Statistics, DZS), 2001–2021, whole-population denominator; county is the finest geography with complete three-wave tables.
Czechia 1 wave · 2021
Regional (kraj) census religion, 2021; earlier waves used an incompatible write-in classification and are deferred.
Denmark 16 waves · 2011–2026membership register
Municipality and parish Church of Denmark administrative membership register overlays; parish detail ships 2023–2026 only, pending a concordance for earlier waves.
Estonia 3 waves · 2000–2021
County census religious affiliation among the population aged 15 and over (Statistics Estonia), 2000–2021, each wave on its own period county vintage; no cross-vintage change metric, and 2021 religion comes from the census sample survey.
Germany 2 waves · 2011–2022
Kreis Zensus legal public-law church membership overlays, 2011–2022, each wave on its own period boundary vintage; no cross-vintage change metric.
Ghana 2 waves · 2010–2021
Regional census religious-affiliation overlays, 2010–2021 with a wave-to-wave change metric, plus a pre-2019 ten-region companion; district religion is deferred.
Hungary 3 waves · 2001–2022
County census religion (Hungarian Central Statistical Office, KSH), 2001–2022; a voluntary question with shares among stated responses, and non-response grew from 10.8% to 40.1% across the waves — cross-wave changes may reflect changing respondent composition as well as changing affiliation.
Iceland 29 waves · 1998–2026membership register
National register membership in religious and life-stance organisations (Statistics Iceland), 1998–2026; a register construct published as a national total only, measuring neither census affiliation, belief, nor practice. “No religious organisation” keeps the source name and is not a no-religion measure.
India 2 waves · 2001–2011
District census religious-community overlays, 2001–2011, each wave on its own period districts (no no-religion category; no 2021 census).
Iran 3 waves · 2006–2016
Province-concordance census religion (Statistical Centre of Iran, SCI), 2006–2016, on 28 analytical units; the record uses the SCI recognised categories, and identities outside them, including unrecognised religious minorities, do not appear. No explicit SCI reuse licence located; published with attribution under project-lead approval.
Ireland 3 waves · 2011–2022
County-and-city census religious-affiliation overlays, 2011–2022.
Israel 8 waves · 1948–2024register
District Population Register religious classification (Israel CBS), 1948–2024; a register classification, not belief or practice, and “not classified by religion” is not a no-religion measure. 1948 is context only.
Italy 24 years · 2001–2025attendance survey
Region self-reported religious-attendance survey estimates (ISTAT AVQ), 2001–2025; a practice construct, not census affiliation and not comparable to a count.
Kenya 1 wave · 2019
County census religious-affiliation overlays, 2019; 2009 religion was published at national level only.
Liechtenstein 3 waves · 2010–2020
Municipality census religious affiliation (Statistics Liechtenstein), 2010–2020; self-declared affiliation that measures neither belief nor practice. The source's German category labels carry English display labels, and the 2020 Protestant label is documented unharmonised.
Lithuania 3 waves · 2001–2021
Municipality census religion (State Data Agency), 2001–2021; the 2021 wave is a sample and model based survey estimate on the census denominator, and no change metric crosses that instrument break.
Malawi 1 wave · 2018
District census religious-affiliation overlays, 2018; 2008 religion was published at national level only.
Mauritius 3 waves · 2000–2022
District census self-reported religion (Statistics Mauritius), 2000–2022; religion is never an ethnic proxy, and an explicit no-religion share exists for 2022 only.
Mexico 3 waves · 2000–2020
Municipality census religion overlays, 2010–2020 (four-construct denominator).
Netherlands 6 waves · 2010–2015survey series
Province self-reported religious-involvement survey (CBS 83288NED), 2010–2015; affiliation and attendance constructs, a sample survey with no confidence interval, discontinued after 2015.
New Zealand 3 waves · 2013–2023
Territorial-authority and statistical-area-2 census overlays, 2013–2023.
Norway 21 waves · 2005–2025membership register
Diocese Church of Norway membership register counts (Statistics Norway), 2005–2025. SSB publishes no diocese population denominator; the map shows counts only. The register records administrative membership; it does not record census affiliation, attendance, or belief.
Palestine 3 waves · 1997–2017
Governorate census religious affiliation (Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics, PCBS), 1997–2017; the source's own Jerusalem J1 coverage differs across waves, and the waves are separate snapshots with no change metric.
Poland 10 waves · 2014–2024attendance count
Diocese Catholic Mass-attendance head count (ISKK dominicantes, communicantes), 2014–2024 (2020 not counted); a practice count, not census affiliation. 17 of 41 diocese boundaries are approximate digitisations, seven flagged low confidence.
Portugal 2 waves · 2011–2021
Municipality census religion overlays, 2011–2021 (residents aged 15+; earlier waves deferred).
Romania 2 waves · 2011–2021
County census religion 2011–2021 with commune detail (small-count cells suppressed at commune level).
Rwanda 2 waves · 2012–2022
District census religion 2012–2022 on the 30 post-2006 districts; 2002 (twelve-prefecture geography) deferred.
Saint Lucia 3 waves · 2001–2022
District census religion (Central Statistical Office), 2001–2022; the source's own category cells differ from its printed district totals by one to three people and ship unchanged with disclosure, the 2022 wave is Provisional Results, and cross-wave change is withheld because the category frames differ.
Serbia 3 waves · 2002–2022
Census religion for the City of Belgrade plus 24 districts (okruzi), 2002–2022 (SORS); the product follows SORS's published territorial coverage, which excludes data for the Autonomous Province of Kosovo and Metohija.
Singapore 2 waves · 2010–2020
Planning-area census religion (SingStat), 2010 and 2020, each wave on its own Master Plan boundary vintage; no cross-wave change metric.
Slovakia 3 waves · 2001–2021
Municipality and city-part census religion, 2021, plus a kraj (region) companion adding 2001 and 2011; municipality-level earlier waves are deferred.
South Africa 3 waves · 1996–2022
Province census religion overlays, 1996–2022 (the 2011 census dropped religion; the 2016 Community Survey is a separate survey construct and is deferred).
South Korea 3 waves · 1995–2015
Municipal (si/gun/gu) census religion 2005–2015, plus a current-sido series adding 1995 (2015 is the 20% sample survey; religion last asked 2015).
Switzerland 4+15 waves · 1970–2024census + survey
Canton religious affiliation from two instruments: full-count census 1970–2000 and annual structural survey 2010–2024; separate levels, no change metric across them.
Taiwan 5 waves · 2020–2024register
County/city administrative register of registered temples and churches and reported temple followers (Ministry of the Interior), 2020–2024; a register construct, never census affiliation, belief, or attendance. The map shows reported temple followers and their year-on-year change; from 2014 the follower column counts temple followers only.
Tonga 1 wave · 2021
District census religious-affiliation overlays, 2021, from the Tonga Statistics Department; refused responses stay in the denominator and the two headline shares do not add to 100. Earlier official waves need a separate comparability extraction and are deferred.
Tuvalu 2 waves · 2012–2017
Region census religious affiliation (Central Statistics Division), 2012–2017, for Funafuti and the Outer Islands; near-universal affiliation makes the two headline shares vary little between regions, and the 2017 wave is a mini-census. The 2022 census reports religion nationally only; it is not mapped by region, and no change metric is offered.
United Kingdom 3 waves · 2001–2021
Local-authority census religion: England & Wales 2001–2021, Northern Ireland 2021; Scotland boundary-ready, extraction pending.
United States 15 waves · 1850–2020religious bodies
County seating, members, or adherents per 100 population, 1850–2020 (not a census affiliation question).
Vanuatu 4 waves · 1967–2020
Province and area-council census religion overlays, 1999–2020.
Zambia 2 waves · 2010–2022
Province census religion overlays, 2010–2022 (2010 percent-only; district religion is published only as a bundled remainder and is deferred).
