Review boundary

Use accepted for export only when the evidence draft is ready for the governed export and pow validation path. Accepting for export records the review snapshot shown on this page; a PI acceptance checks that snapshot again and is refused if the evidence or its confirmed locations changed since you decided. Reload the task and decide again if that happens.

Use unresolved notes for useful but incomplete RA submissions. An unresolved note keeps the lead open on the task until a reviewer asks for more evidence, defers it, rejects it, or accepts it after review.

Use needs more evidence when the RA should clarify a source, date, identity match, or lifecycle claim (a dated event in the place's life: worship beginning, ending, moving, or the building changing use).
