# Data access and research tiers

Status: revised 2026-07-13.

The project separates what it shows from what it shares. The public repository and placesmap.org carry the map: the country research maps, the derived area summaries that feed them, the boundary layers under their stated licences, and the platform (portal, review tools, shared runtime). The project does not redistribute census data: assembled datasets serve the team's own analysis and publications only.

During the current work phase, the project's research workflows — source assessments, provenance manifests, extraction pipelines, and planning records — are held in a private research tier alongside the assembled data. Collaborators receive access by invitation; others are welcome to request specific provenance detail through the contact on the project README. Every map surface continues to name its source, its construct, and its licence position directly on the page.

This arrangement is a work-phase posture, not the project's final shape.
