## 2. What was reproduced

All five were reproduced by reading the code and, for 1, 3, 4, and 5, by driving the built portal in Chrome on a simulated signed-in session (the recipe from the handover: `backend`, `backendUser`, `renderBackendPanel()`, `setPortalMode("add")`, then the pin and revise flows). Before the change, add mode stacked the add-place control, its long help, My work, the nomination help, and only then the pin host with the address search; the signed-in card carried the batch name and the task counts throughout. The rapid form's four current-use options carried one-line notes that did not say what "used for worship" means and had no option for a place that is gone. The pin flow offered "Cancel" on the confirm card, "Cancel" on the form, and "Discard entry" on the form; the revise pane offered "Cancel and return to the map". The revise route ran the pin flow and the rapid current-observation form and offered dates only afterwards, as *Add where and when* on the confirmation screen and on the recorded-observation card, so an RA revising a place never met the period cards before submitting.

Finding 2 was reproduced in code: `applyTypedCoordinates` parsed the two coordinate boxes only, so "Move pin" pressed after an address search with the boxes empty wrote a small status line and moved nothing; the search results themselves moved the pin only when one was clicked. `setPendingPin` also returned silently once a location was confirmed. Entering pin mode did not release the selected task.

## 3. What changed

1. **Entry focus.** A `body.entry-open` class, set when a pin flow, a task's guided form, the periods pane, or the history pane opens and cleared when the sidebar returns to rest, hides the task list, My work, the nominations panel, the filters, the stats, the quickstart banner, the mode notice, the long help under the add-place control, and the batch name, counts, refresh, and sign-out on the signed-in card. The signed-in line stays as one sentence with a "Show task list" link that restores the panels without discarding anything. The pin host moved in the HTML to sit directly under "Add a missing place", so the address search and the coordinate boxes are the next thing on screen; while the pin flow is armed (`body.pin-open`) the detail panel's resting intro leaves too. A new entry releases the previously selected task.
2. **Move pin.** With both coordinate boxes empty, "Move pin" moves the pin to the first search result still on screen and says so; the result buttons read "Move pin here: …"; with nothing to move on the status says what to do; after the location is confirmed a move is refused with "The location is already confirmed. Discard this entry to place the pin again." Typed coordinates work as before. Tested in `apps/regions/nz/js/portal-walkthrough-dom.test.cjs` through the real handler.
3. **Use guidance and the past-existence option.** Each current-use option carries a one-line helper. "This site is used for worship" reads: at the observation date the place exists and worship happens here at any known frequency; give the frequency in the periods below; a place used once a year is recorded as in use at an intermittent level, never as uncertain (ruling R-F1′, section 7). The guided form's action select carries the same guidance. A fifth option, "This place existed but no longer exists" (`place_no_longer_exists`), derives existence `absent`, worship use `not_worship`, action `closed_or_changed_use`; the server vocabulary, the derivation table, the consistency check, and the tests carry it. Convex deploys before the static merge for this value.
4. **Cancel and discard.** "Back to map (keeps your draft)" leaves the form with the device draft kept; "Discard this entry (deletes it)" keeps its confirmation; "Cancel placement" drops an unconfirmed pin; the revise pane's way out is "Back to the map"; the issue form's is "Close without filing". The guide says what each does.
5. **Dates on revise (and on a nomination).** The period cards and the chain block sit inside the rapid form, before its buttons, under "Dates, denomination, and changes" — open for a revision, collapsed for a new place. They keep their own device key (`rapid-pin-periods`, per country and user), anchor their still-in-use card to the observation date, take the confirmed pin as "same place as the pin", and default to their own source and confidence because a rapid observation carries no assessment confidence; ticking "same source" takes the direct-observation note as their source account and is refused when that note is short, never padded. On submit the cards are validated first (a bad set stops the whole submission), the observation goes, and the periods are recorded against the new parent through the existing `submitOccupancies` route, which already accepts a rapid parent; the confirmation screen counts them. If that second write fails the observation stands, the cards are kept, and *Add where and when* opens the pane with them restored. Discarding the entry clears the cards; sign-out clears them with every other set. The post-submission pane remains the second route. No new Convex function was needed for this finding.

## 7. Ruling R-F1′ (JB, 2026-09-03), built here

JB's reasoning: "uncertain" is epistemic (we do not know), while an annual service is certain knowledge of rare use, and rarity can be the significance; keep the two axes apart. Presence stays present / absent / uncertain. A period with `use_frequency` `annual` or `occasional` derives present with the same certainty as any present year; only `use_frequency: uncertain` derives an uncertain presence, and rule 11 `intermittent_use` keeps its id for that case. A new derived field per census year, `use_level` (`regular` | `intermittent`; omitted when the presence is uncertain), rides on `derived_target_year_states` and its segment firings, shows in the reviewer panel as a pill beside the status and is confirmed with the presence into `target_year_use_levels` on the parent, exports as `target_year_<Y>_use_level` beside the status (server list, portal mirror, and the CSV template moved together; NZ wide row 125 columns), and the preview strip reads "2018 present, intermittent (inside the period, annual use)". The derivation version is `occupancy_derivation_v3`, so the first resubmission on a task returns its confirmed presence years to unconfirmed once. The Kohekohe fictional continuation derives 2013 present regular / Presbyterian, 2018 present intermittent, 2023 present intermittent; no denomination after the desacralisation is unchanged. Recorded in `docs/development/function-chain-brief-2026-09-02.md` §5a, amended in place in `docs/development/place-of-worship-definition-2026-09-03-v0.1.5.md` (its revision note carries the amendment; no v0.1.6, since the version had not been cited outside the repository), and in `docs/api/convex-functions.md`.
