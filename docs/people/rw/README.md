# Instructions for RW — historical mission-station data and review

Welcome Robert — this page is the durable copy of how your historical
places-of-worship material enters the project and what your portal
roles let you do. It supersedes any email version.

## Where your work fits

The pilot corpus is your historical mission-station data for Vanuatu.
Stations enter the shared backend through the curator batch-import
lane: one file, one source record — the source is registered once in
the shared source register and every station entry carries a reference
to it plus its own locator (page or entry number). Imported rows arrive
as drafts, never as accepted records; every station then passes human
review before it can reach any export.

Two standing rules shape the station entries:

- **Named tenures only where a death is recorded.** Personal names
  (ministers, missionaries, teachers) are published only when the
  person's death is on record; otherwise the tenure is recorded without
  the name. A recorded death date is the only accepted evidence of
  decease.
- **Dates carry their uncertainty.** Openings, closures, and
  relocations are recorded with explicit bounds and a stated basis
  ("founding stated", "first seen only", and so on) — a first-seen date
  never manufactures an absence before it.

## Your portal roles

Your account carries both contributor and reviewer roles:

- **[Vanuatu entry portal](https://religionmap.org/apps/regions/vu/verification.html)** —
  add places, record observations, attach photos and documents
  (attachments are review-only, never public).
- **[Vanuatu review portal](https://religionmap.org/apps/regions/vu/review.html)** —
  the review queue. Claim a task ("claimed by me" keeps two reviewers
  off the same submission), decide with a short note, or call for a
  second opinion before acceptance. One rule is enforced by the server:
  you cannot review your own submissions — any other qualified reviewer
  can.

`Accepted for export` means accepted into the reviewed export bundle —
NOT "on the map". Nothing reaches the master data or the public map
until it passes the governed validation path and a rebuild. Every
decision is revisable; nothing in the backend is ever deleted.

## Sending data

Email files to JB; he runs the import and returns a per-row report
(imported / rejected / duplicate-skipped). Cite the source on each
file (title, year, page range), keep the source's own names and
spellings as printed, put your readings or harmonisations in a
separate column beside them, and leave missing values blank. Every
delivery is hashed, archived durably, and credited to you in the data
manifests and changelog — credit is public by design; contact details
never enter the repository.
