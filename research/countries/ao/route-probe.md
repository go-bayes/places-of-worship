# Angola census/survey religion route probe

Verified 2026-07-12. PROBE ONLY — no build. **Verdict: REFUTED-PREMISE (religion published nationally only in both sources); a religion-by-province product is HELD behind the standing restricted-microdata hold.** Angola asks religion in both instruments the queue row implicates — the 2014 census (RGPH 2014) and the IIMS 2015-2016 health survey — but publishes it **nationally only** in both. The RGPH 2014 definitive-results report carries religion as a single national chart (Gráfico 16, percentages, whole resident population) and lists no religion table among its numbered result tables; the IIMS 2015-2016 final report carries religion as a national background-characteristics distribution (Quadro 3.1, reproductive-age respondents, weighted percent and unweighted count) and never crosses it with province. Neither published source supplies religion by province. The only path to religion-by-province is a custom tabulation from the census or IIMS microdata, and the microdata route is CLOSED under the project's restricted-microdata hold (the IIMS is DHS-program-implemented; the census microdata is held in the same posture). The boundary is clean and licensed — geoBoundaries AGO ADM1, eighteen provinces, Public Domain, matching the 2014/2015-16 eighteen-province frame one-to-one — but a licensed boundary cannot join to a religion-by-province table that no published source contains. The queue premise ("provinces, urban/rural | official health and indicators survey ... probe then build") is refuted for religion: the IIMS does break many health indicators by province and by urban/rural, but religion is not one of them.

## Premise correction (trust the record)

The queue row 107 reads: `Angola (AO) | 2015–2016 | provinces, urban/rural | official health and indicators survey | browser work | probe then build`. The record corrects its central premise.

The premise the record refutes is that the "official health and indicators survey" yields religion by province. The IIMS 2015-2016 (Inquérito de Indicadores Múltiplos e de Saúde) does tabulate its health, demographic, and knowledge indicators by province and by urban/rural residence, and it does record each respondent's religion. But religion appears in the published final report only as a national background characteristic — a row category used to disaggregate other indicators, and a national composition table for women and men aged 15-49. No table in the report crosses religion with province. The parallel census route (RGPH 2014) is the same shape: religion asked, published national-only. So the buildable object the queue names — religious affiliation by province — does not exist in any published Angola source; it exists only in the microdata, which the project holds closed.

### RGPH 2014 census — religion is a national chart, not a table

The definitive-results report (`Resultados Definitivos do Censo 2014`, INE, 19 March 2016; the "Março 2016 Relatório" on the INE publications portal is the same file) presents religion once, at section 7.1.11, as **Gráfico 16 — "Principais religiões praticadas, 2014"**. Verbatim national percentages, read from the chart: Católica 41,1; Protestante 38,1; Sem religião 12,3; Outra 7,4; Animista 0,6; Islâmica 0,4; Judaica 0,2. The narrative states, verbatim: *"Em 2014, a religião católica é a principal religião praticada em Angola, com 41% da população; a seguir aparece a religião protestante com 38%."* (In 2014, the Catholic religion is the main religion practised in Angola, at 41% of the population; the Protestant religion follows at 38%.) The report's numbered result tables (Quadro 1 through Quadro 33) cover population, age, marital status, birth registration, disability, orphanhood, literacy, education, media, economic activity, housing, water, sanitation, electricity, waste, and agriculture — **none is religion**, and none crosses religion with the province variable. Chapters 6-8 present the main tables, graphs, and cartograms "a nível nacional e provincial", but religion sits only in the national graph, with no provincial counterpart. Grain for religion: national, percentages, no counts, no province.

The census provincial frame is **eighteen provinces** (Quadro 4 lists Angola plus the eighteen: Cabinda, Zaire, Uíge, Luanda, Cuanza Norte, Cuanza Sul, Malanje, Lunda Norte, Benguela, Huambo, Bié, Moxico, Cuando Cubango, Namibe, Huíla, Cunene, Lunda Sul, Bengo). National resident population 25,789,024.

### IIMS 2015-2016 — religion is a national background characteristic, restricted to respondents

The final report (`Inquérito de Indicadores Múltiplos e de Saúde (IIMS)`, INE/MINSA, June 2017) presents religion as a background characteristic in **Quadro 3.1 "Características dos homens e mulheres entrevistados"** — the distribution of women and men aged 15-49 by selected characteristics, national, weighted percent plus weighted and unweighted counts. Verbatim (women / men, weighted percent): Católica 41,5 / 37,8; Assembleia de Deus 9,7 / 3,3; Metodista 3,7 / 2,2; Universal 2,0 / 2,9; Testemunhas de Jeová 3,5 / 6,1; Protestante 33,5 / 30,2; Islâmica 0,3 / 0,4; Animista 0,4 / 0,8; Sem religião 5,2 / 16,3; Outro 0,2 / 0,0. Elsewhere in the report religion appears as a **row breakdown of other indicators** (contraceptive use, antenatal coverage, HIV knowledge, tobacco use, and the like) — always national, never a religion-by-province cross-tab. The report does carry many province-disaggregated tables for its health indicators (eighteen provinces as a breakdown dimension) and urban/rural splits, which is what the queue row's "provinces, urban/rural" refers to; religion is simply not among the province-broken variables.

Two survey-construct cautions the project's rules impose. The IIMS religion figure is a **survey estimate** over the reproductive-age respondent universe (women 15-49; the men's table shown is 15-49), not the whole-population census universe; it carries a sample design (33 primary sampling units per province, 66 in Luanda; 16,302 households selected) and would need weights and sampling uncertainty for any estimate. A province-by-religion estimate would also hit the small-cell rule hard: religion has ten categories and, split across eighteen provinces on a per-province sample of roughly 33 clusters, many province×religion cells would fall below any reasonable reporting threshold and would carry wide confidence intervals or be unreportable. Even were the microdata open, a province×religion survey product would be fragile.

### RGPH 2024 census — no religion in the published synthesis

Angola completed a new census in 2024 (RGPH 2024; definitive results released from November 2025, provincial reports appearing into February 2026) on the **new twenty-one-province frame** created by the 2024 administrative reorganisation (Icolo e Bengo, Cuando, Cubango, Moxico Leste, Cassai-Zambeze split from the former eighteen). The national synthesis report (`Relatório Síntese dos Resultados Definitivos, RGPH 2024`) contains **no religion content** (a `religi` scan of the full extraction returns nothing). Whether the 2024 census asked religion, and whether a fuller 2024 thematic or provincial volume will publish it by province, is not established by the synthesis; that is a forward-looking item, not a present route. Note the frame break: any 2024 religion product would be on twenty-one provinces, not the eighteen that the 2014/2015-16 sources and the geoBoundaries layer use.

## Why a build is blocked

A religion-by-province product needs religion tabulated to the province. No published Angola source supplies it: the census publishes religion as a national chart, the IIMS as a national background characteristic. The only object that carries religion at province grain is the **microdata** — the RGPH 2014 census microdata (INE ANDINE catalog, World Bank microdata catalog #2870) and the IIMS 2015-2016 individual recodes (DHS-program-implemented). Both are closed under the project's standing restricted-microdata hold; the probe brief states the microdata route is CLOSED, and no DHS/IPUMS download or registration is permitted. Under RENDER THE RECORD the missing province cells cannot be invented, allocated, or backcast from the national margin. The route is therefore refuted on published grain and held on the microdata.

## Small-cell considerations

If the microdata hold were ever lifted, two small-cell constraints apply. The census route (whole-population counts) would be a clean province×religion count table with no sampling uncertainty, and small cells would be genuine structural counts (Islâmica and Judaica are each below 0.5% nationally and would be very thin in most provinces). The IIMS route is a survey and is the harder case: a per-province sample of roughly 33 clusters split across ten religion categories would leave many province×religion cells below a reportable threshold, demanding weights, design-based standard errors, and category collapsing — a fragile product the project would treat as a survey construct distinct from the census counts, never merged with them.

## Blockers and the exact unblock

- **Published grain (the genuine gate)**: religion is published nationally only, in both the RGPH 2014 census (Gráfico 16, national percentages) and the IIMS 2015-2016 report (Quadro 3.1, national respondent composition). No published source carries religion by province. This is the REFUTED premise.
- **Microdata hold (the policy gate)**: the only province-grain religion route is a custom tabulation from the RGPH 2014 census microdata or the IIMS 2015-2016 individual recodes. Both are closed under the project's restricted-microdata hold (the IIMS is DHS-program-implemented). This is the HELD path.
- **Licence (secondary)**: INE carries no open reuse grant; both site surfaces are all-rights-reserved (`consent-first`). Any future product would need an INE reuse permission bundled with the data request.
- **Unblock**: an INE reuse-and-data request for a **religion-by-province table (RGPH 2014 census counts, eighteen provinces)** — a census tabulation INE can produce internally without releasing microdata, and the cleaner of the two sources (whole-population counts, no sampling uncertainty). Failing that, a conductor/PI ruling lifting the microdata hold for the census recodes specifically. The IIMS survey route is the weaker fallback (respondent universe, small province×religion cells, design-based uncertainty). The 2024 census is a forward watch: if a fuller RGPH 2024 volume publishes religion by the new twenty-one provinces, that becomes a fresh (frame-shifted) route.
- **Recommended queue-row text**: `probed 2026-07-12, REFUTED-PREMISE: religion is asked in both the RGPH 2014 census and the IIMS 2015-16 health survey but published NATIONALLY ONLY in both — census as a national chart (Gráfico 16: Católica 41.1%, Protestante 38.1%, Sem religião 12.3%), IIMS as a national background characteristic (Quadro 3.1, respondents 15-49). Neither publishes religion by province; the province route exists only in microdata (census #2870 / IIMS DHS recodes), CLOSED under the restricted-microdata hold. Boundary geoBoundaries AGO ADM1 clean (18 provinces, Public Domain, matches the 2014/2015-16 frame). Licence consent-first (INE all-rights-reserved, no open grant). 2024 census on new 21-province frame, no religion in its synthesis. Unblock: INE request for a census religion-by-province count table, or a PI ruling on the census microdata ([route-probe](countries/ao/route-probe.md))`.

## Recommendation

Do not build. Angola's published record supplies religion at the national level only, in both the 2014 census and the 2015-2016 IIMS, so the queued religion-by-province product cannot be assembled from published tables; the sole province-grain route is microdata, which the project holds closed. The boundary (geoBoundaries AGO ADM1, eighteen provinces, Public Domain) and the census frame agree eighteen-to-eighteen and are build-ready the moment a province-grain religion table exists. The single highest-value unblock is an INE request for a RGPH 2014 religion-by-province count table — a product INE can generate without releasing microdata — paired with a reuse permission that resolves the all-rights-reserved licence. Absent that, mark the row REFUTED-PREMISE with the microdata path recorded as HELD.
