## Build recipe

Do not build a Belgium religion-over-time map until an official subnational source or reusable church administrative source is documented.

## Risks and open questions

Belgium recognises religious bodies, but recognition and funding data are not population affiliation. National surveys cannot support a subnational longitudinal map.

## Deep-history potential

Moderate to high. State archives, diocesan archives, parish registers, synagogue records, mosque directories, monument inventories, and local gazetteers can support site histories even without modern census religion.
