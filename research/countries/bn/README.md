## First visualisation

Do not build a subnational affiliation map from current public evidence.

## Build recipe

No build recipe for affiliation mapping. Reopen only if DEPS releases district-level religion tables for 2011, 2021, or a later round.

## Risks and open questions

Religion is tied to state identity and regulation in Brunei. Public mapping should avoid treating administrative permission, worship-place presence, and personal affiliation as interchangeable.

## Deep-history potential

Medium. Brunei census reports, National Archives of Brunei holdings, Ministry of Religious Affairs mosque registers, church records, colonial residency records, and district reports can support a site-focused historical project.
