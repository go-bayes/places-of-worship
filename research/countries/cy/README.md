## Build recipe

Do not build a Cyprus religion-over-time map until a district or municipality religion table is documented.

## Risks and open questions

The located census religion tables are useful national summaries, but national totals cannot support a subnational map. Political geography and territorial coverage require explicit documentation before any Cyprus product.

## Deep-history potential

High. State archives, Church of Cyprus records, vakif records, mosque registers, Armenian and Maronite community records, heritage inventories, cadastral records, and newspapers can support deeper site histories.
