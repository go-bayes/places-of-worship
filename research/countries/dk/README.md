## First visualisation

Map current Church of Denmark membership share by parish, then add annual national or parish waves only after the longitudinal source path is documented.

## Build recipe

Start with a reproducible scrape or download for parish membership and DAGI parish polygons. Record state-church membership as its own construct. Do not treat it as census religion or attendance.

## Risks and open questions

The source covers Church of Denmark membership. It does not measure all religious affiliation. Historic parish boundary changes and parish mergers need crosswalks.

## Deep-history potential

High. Danish church books, parish registers, kirkestatistik, National Archives holdings, local histories, cemetery records, and historic maps can support deeper site histories.
