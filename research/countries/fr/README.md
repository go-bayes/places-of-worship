## Build recipe

Do not build a France census-religion map. Any France work should be framed as a places-of-worship inventory or a survey analysis with access and disclosure limits.

## Risks and open questions

French legal and statistical practice treats religion as sensitive and excludes it from the census. Survey estimates must not be presented as census counts.

## Deep-history potential

High. Archives departementales, diocesan archives, parish registers, synagogue records, Protestant consistorial records, mosque association records, heritage inventories, Gallica newspapers, and Cassini resources can support site histories.
