## Risks and open questions

The located source is valuable for a national annual series, but national-only data do not meet the map brief. Organisation membership is also not attendance or census affiliation.

## Deep-history potential

Moderate to high. National Archives of Iceland, parish registers, church books, Lutheran records, local histories, heritage registers, and newspapers can support deeper site histories.
