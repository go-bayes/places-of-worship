## First visualisation

Do not build a religion-affiliation map from current public evidence.

## Build recipe

No build recipe for affiliation mapping. Reopen only if the Department of Statistics releases governorate-level religion tables or an official administrative construct is approved.

## Risks and open questions

Religion mapping in Jordan can expose small Christian and other minority communities and can intersect with refugee-status geography. Public maps should not disaggregate national estimates without official subnational data.

## Deep-history potential

Medium. Ottoman tax and population registers, Department of Statistics census reports, Jordan National Library and archive holdings, Ministry of Awqaf mosque records, church directories, and Mandate-era records can support historical work.
