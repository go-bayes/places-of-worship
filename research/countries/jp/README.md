## First visualisation

Do not build a religion-affiliation map. A separate prefecture map of registered religious corporations could be considered with a clear administrative-organisation label.

## Build recipe

No build recipe for affiliation mapping. Reopen only if an official, subnational affiliation source is identified; otherwise scope Japan as a registered-organisation and site-inventory case.

## Risks and open questions

Administrative religious-corporation counts do not measure personal affiliation or practice. Japan also has overlapping Shinto, Buddhist, Christian, new-religion, and folk-practice participation that a single-affiliation map would misrepresent.

## Deep-history potential

High. Agency for Cultural Affairs religious-corporation statistics, prefectural registers, National Diet Library holdings, temple parishioner registers, shrine records, Meiji and later local gazetteers, and cultural-property registers can support a strong site-history project.
