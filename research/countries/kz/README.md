## Risks and open questions

Religion data in Kazakhstan intersect with state registration, ethnicity, migration, and high non-response or undeclared categories. Public maps should avoid interpreting region-level affiliation as evidence of practice or institutional presence.

## Deep-history potential

Medium-high. The 1897 Russian Empire census, Soviet census materials, Kazakhstan national census reports, National Archives holdings, registered religious-association records, mosque records, and Orthodox diocesan records can support long-run histories.
