## First visualisation

Do not build a religion-affiliation map from current public evidence.

## Build recipe

No build recipe for affiliation mapping. Reopen only if the Lao Statistics Bureau releases province-level religion tables for 2015, 2023, or another census round.

## Risks and open questions

The public census categories include Buddhist, Christian, no religion, and other categories that may mask local traditions. A site layer would need caution in minority and state-regulated religious settings.

## Deep-history potential

Medium. French Indochina records, Lao National Archives holdings, Buddhist temple records, Ministry or home-affairs religious-organisation registrations, missionary records, and provincial gazetteers can support a historical site track.
