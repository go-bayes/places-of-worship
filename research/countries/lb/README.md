## First visualisation

Do not build a modern religion-affiliation map. A historical 1932-only map would need a separate archival and ethics review.

## Build recipe

No build recipe for modern affiliation mapping. Reopen only under a specifically approved historical-confessional project with clear source and risk controls.

## Risks and open questions

Lebanon is a high-sensitivity exclusion because sectarian balance is politically central and no modern census has replaced the 1932 frame. Public small-area religion mapping could create harm even where sources appear public.

## Deep-history potential

High but sensitive. The 1932 census, Ottoman salnames and tapu records, French Mandate records, church registers, waqf records, community archives, and district histories can support restricted historical research.
