## Build recipe

Do not build a Luxembourg religion-over-time map from census data. A separate places-of-worship inventory could use OSM and denominational directories without claiming population affiliation.

## Risks and open questions

Legal non-collection makes the missing census series substantive. The census series is not merely undiscovered. Survey or directory sources would measure different constructs.

## Deep-history potential

Moderate. National Archives of Luxembourg, diocesan archives, parish registers, synagogue records, commune archives, monument inventories, and newspapers can support site histories.
