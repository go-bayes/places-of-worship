## Build recipe

Start with the 2014 census `Thematic Report on Religion` state/region table and geoBoundaries MMR ADM1. Treat the product as a contextual baseline rather than a longitudinal series.

## Risks and open questions

Religion data in Myanmar carry acute risk because census exclusions, Rohingya classification, conflict displacement, and local minority safety affect both counts and interpretation. Public mapping may be inappropriate below state/region level.

## Deep-history potential

Medium. British Burma census volumes, National Archives of Myanmar holdings, monastery records, church mission records, mosque registers, and colonial district gazetteers provide historical routes, but access and political risk are substantial.
