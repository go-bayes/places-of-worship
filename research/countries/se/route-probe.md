# Sweden Church-of-Sweden membership route probe

Verified 2026-07-12. Verdict: **HELD for a subnational map; the national single-denomination membership series is separately buildable but licence-unstated.** Sweden has no census religion; every route is administrative church membership. The Church of Sweden (Svenska kyrkan) holds the membership register and publishes a clean national series 1972-2025, delivered as a PDF with an intact text layer (7,754,784 members and 95.2% of the population in 1972; 5,365,542 and 50.7% in 2025). Diocese totals are published on each diocese's own "i siffror" page. Membership per parish (församling) and per municipality (kommun) is compiled by the church — the news services that publish parish-level maps state plainly that the church supplied it — but no public machine-readable per-parish or per-municipality membership file was located in this probe, and the church statistics page carries no reuse licence. The other faith-community route is the state grant agency's served-population count (SST, now folded into the youth-and-civil-society agency MUCF): a national-only annual series 2020-2024 for about sixty communities, delivered as on-page tables with no download link and no stated licence. Boundaries are clean: SCB municipality and county vectors publish under CC0, church parish boundaries publish through the Church of Sweden API portal (mostly CC BY 4.0, © Svenska kyrkan), and Lantmäteriet's Distrikt division (about 2,523 units frozen on the 1999-12-31 parish boundaries) publishes under CC0. A subnational Sweden product is possible along the Norway diocese and Denmark folkekirken precedents (both BUILT: single-church membership on official polygons), blocked on two items — a located per-unit membership data file and a church-statistics licence ruling.

## Build decision (recommendation to the conductor)

- **National church-membership series (the church PDF)**: **national only, 1972-2025, buildable after a clean PDF text extract, licence-unstated.** Single-denomination register construct (member count and member % of population). No browser work; `pdftotext -layout` recovered the full table. Whether a national-only Sweden ships is a conductor or PI call, as in Finland — Sweden (10.5 million, large territory) is not the Iceland small-country case. The one open item even for the national series is reuse licence.
- **Subnational church membership (the genuine subnational route)**: **HELD.** Diocese totals are published per diocese (HTML); per-parish and per-municipality membership is compiled by the church and supplied to media, but no public machine-readable file was located. Named unblocks below: a per-unit data file plus a church-statistics licence.
- **SST/MUCF served-population series (other faith communities)**: **national only, 2020-2024.** About sixty communities, "betjänade" (served) count that mixes members with regular non-member participants. On-page tables, no download, no stated licence. National only — not a subnational route.
- **Boundaries**: **clean and available.** SCB municipality/county CC0; church parish boundaries CC BY 4.0 / © Svenska kyrkan via the API portal; Lantmäteriet Distrikt (1999 parish frame) CC0. SCB also ships population per parish (2004-2025, CC0) as the membership-share denominator.

## The national route: church PDF, national only, clean text layer

The Church of Sweden statistics page, "Svenska kyrkan i siffror" (`https://www.svenskakyrkan.se/statistik`, retrieved 2026-07-12), links a set of national-total PDFs and one parish/diocese XLSX (see below). The membership series is `Medlemmar i Svenska kyrkan 1972-2025.pdf` (`https://www.svenskakyrkan.se/filer/1374643/Medlemmar%20i%20Svenska%20kyrkan%201972-2025.pdf?id=3016165`, three pages, text layer intact). It carries one row per year with three columns — member count, members as a percentage of the population, and the year-on-year change in that percentage:

| Year | Members | % of population | Δ pp |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1972 | 7,754,784 | 95.2 | — |
| 1980 | 7,690,636 | 92.9 | −0.2 |
| 2000 | 7,360,825 (series continues) | — | — |
| 2020 | 5,728,748 | 55.2 | −1.2 |
| 2024 | 5,426,053 | 51.4 | −1.1 |
| 2025 | 5,365,542 | 50.7 | −1.1 |

The 1981 row is blank: the source note states that the Riksskatteverket compiled no personförteckning that year, therefore no population base was available ("År 1981 upprättades ingen personförteckning av Riksskatteverket, varför inga befolkningdata kunde erhållas" — source spelling preserved). The construct is an administrative register, not a survey or a census: since 1996 no child is born into membership, and the percentage is members divided by the total population. The signal is strong — a fall from 95.2% (1972) to 50.7% (2025) — but the table is national. The other page PDFs (`In- och utträden 1970-2025`, `Döpta, konfirmerade, vigda och begravda 1970-2025`, `Gudstjänstbesök 1990-2025`, and others) are national totals in the same style.

## The subnational route: compiled by the church, no public machine-readable file located

Two published grains sit below the national total, and a third exists but was not located as a public file.

- **Diocese (stift) — published, HTML.** Each of the thirteen dioceses runs an "i siffror" page carrying its own membership total. Lunds stift i siffror (`https://www.svenskakyrkan.se/lundsstift/lunds-stift-i-siffror`, 2026-07-12) states 147 parishes and 782,107 members for the diocese. These are on-page figures, not a downloadable table.
- **Parish (församling) and municipality (kommun) — compiled by the church, not located as a public file.** The news services that publish parish-level and municipal membership maps state the source explicitly. Newsworthy's Surahammar parish article (`https://www.newsworthy.se/artikel/244030`, 2026-07-12) prints the source line verbatim: "All statistik kommer från Svenska kyrkan. Befolkningsstatistiken för respektive församling har kyrkan hämtat från SCB." The church therefore holds and distributes membership per parish, with SCB supplying the parish population as the denominator. No public machine-readable per-parish or per-municipality membership file was located on `svenskakyrkan.se/statistik`, on the research-unit page (`https://www.svenskakyrkan.se/forskning/statistik`), or on the diocese pages. The Church of Sweden API portal (below) exposes parish maps and a parish-search API, but no membership-statistics API.
- **Parish/diocese cross-tab, SCB commission — the one located machine-readable per-parish file, a subset.** The statistics page links `Antal sverigefinländare per stift och församling 31 december 2025.xlsx` (`https://www.svenskakyrkan.se/filer/1374643/Antal%20sverigefinl%c3%a4ndare%20per%20stift%20och%20f%c3%b6rsamling%2031%20december%202025%20.xlsx?id=3085875`, 2026-07-12). It is an SCB commissioned delivery (`Uppdrag: 271 488/898 318-1`, `uppdrag@scb.se`, dated 2026-03-13) with four sheets — `Info`, `Riket`, `Stift`, and `Stift_Forsamling`. The `Stift_Forsamling` sheet (27,960 rows) carries `Stiftkod`, `Stift`, `Församlingskod (LKF)`, `Församlingsnamn`, `Åldersgrupp`, `Folkmängd`, and the column `Sverigefinländare som är medlemmar i Svenska kyrkan`. This proves two things: SCB can and does cross church membership with parish (LKF) codes on commission, and the full-population equivalent would take the same shape. The published file covers only the Finnish-background subset, not total membership. A total-membership parish or municipality file is therefore an SCB commission or a church data release away, not an existing public download.

## The SST / MUCF route: served population, national only, other communities

The state grant agency for faith communities published the "betjänade" (served) population per grant-eligible community. The agency, Myndigheten för stöd till trossamfund (SST), was folded into Myndigheten för ungdoms- och civilsamhällesfrågor (MUCF) on 1 January 2026, and its statistics now sit at `mucf.se` (the `myndighetensst.se` bidragsstatistik path 301-redirects to `mucf.se`). The series "Trossamfund - antal betjänade" (`https://www.mucf.se/rapporter-och-statistik/trossamfund-antal-betjanade`, 2026-07-12) is **national only**, covers **2020-2024**, and lists about sixty communities, including two umbrella bodies (the Islamic co-operation council ISR with six congregations, and an Orthodox/Eastern-churches economic council with over twenty). The construct is "betjänad": a member of a community or a person who takes part regularly in its activities; the count therefore mixes members with regular non-member participants and is not a pure membership register. The data appears as on-page HTML tables with no located Excel or CSV download and no stated reuse licence. From 1 January 2025 a community needs at least 2,500 resident served persons to qualify for the grant, which sets the floor for inclusion. This route adds the non-Lutheran communities the church series omits, but only at the national level.

## SCB has no church-membership table (the register sits with the church)

Statistics Sweden's statistical database (PxWeb, `https://api.scb.se/OV0104/v1/doris/en/ssd`, 2026-07-12) has nineteen top-level subject areas and no religion or church area; church membership is not part of Swedish official statistics after the 2000 church-state separation. What SCB does publish, and what a build would use, is the denominator and the geography:

- **Population per parish** — `befolkning-per-forsamling` (`https://www.scb.se/hitta-statistik/regional-statistik-och-kartor/regionala-indelningar/forsamlingar/befolkning-per-forsamling/`, 2026-07-12): `folkmangdperforsamling2004-2014.xlsx` and `befolkning-per-forsamling_from_2015_20260408.xlsx`, 2004-2025, updated each March. SCB notes that only persons registered at a property can be assigned to a parish; those without a property registration attach to a municipality only.
- **Parish organisation and codes** — the `kyrkliga-indelningar` page (`https://www.scb.se/hitta-statistik/regional-statistik-och-kartor/regionala-indelningar/kyrkliga-indelningar/`, 2026-07-12) publishes pastorat-to-parish concordance files (`skp_lkf2027.xlsx`, `skp_lkf2026.xlsx`, `lkf2025.xls`, back to 2021) and diocese/contract/pastorat PDFs. It states that the number of parishes has fallen by over 1,100 since the 2000 separation, and that SCB can hold the parish frame back to 2000-01-01 for time-series work. Parish merges and boundary change dominate the longitudinal problem, exactly as the queue README warns.

## Boundaries

- **Municipality (kommun) and county (län) — CC0.** SCB's digital-boundary coordinate files (`https://www.scb.se/hitta-statistik/regional-statistik-och-kartor/regionala-indelningar/digitala-granser/`, 2026-07-12) ship county, municipality, and LA-region as ArcView-shape and MapInfo-TAB ZIPs (`shape_svenska_260225.zip`, `tab_svenska_260225.zip`). The page states the licence verbatim: "Vi använder licensen CC0 för dessa data, vilket innebär att du får använda och sprida eller tillgängliggöra sådana data utan krav på att ange källa." It also warns these are simplified for thematic mapping and "inte lämpliga för analyser"; an analysis-grade municipal vector would come from Lantmäteriet.
- **Parish (församling) — church API portal, CC BY 4.0 / © Svenska kyrkan.** The Church of Sweden API portal (`https://api.svenskakyrkan.se/`, 2026-07-12) offers `Församlingskartor` — "GIS-lager med församlingsgränser" covering parishes, contracts, pastorats, and dioceses, marked © Svenska kyrkan. The portal front page states most resources now carry CC BY 4.0 ("De flesta resurserna ... går nu under licensen CC BY 4.0") while each API keeps its own agreement to accept ("Varje API har sitt egna avtal som man måste godkänna"). This is the authoritative church-geography source, and the same portal supplies `Församlingssök` (address-to-parish) and `Kyrkobyggnadsregistret` (church-building register).
- **District (distrikt) — Lantmäteriet, CC0.** Lantmäteriet's Distrikt division (about 2,523 units) is a frozen version of the parish boundaries in force on 1999-12-31, published as open data. Lantmäteriet's open-data licence is CC0 (`https://www.lantmateriet.se/oppnadata`, 2026-07-12): "du får använda, sprida, göra om, modifiera och bygga vidare på Lantmäteriets öppna data", with attribution encouraged, not required. The Distrikt frame gives a stable historical parish geometry where current church parishes have merged.

Since 1 January 2020 church parishes need not follow county or municipal boundaries; the pragmatic clean subnational geometry is therefore the municipality (SCB CC0 for thematic mapping, Lantmäteriet CC0 for analysis), with the church `Församlingskartor` layer the route to true parish polygons.

## Construct handling (the single-denomination register class)

Church-of-Sweden membership is a single-denomination administrative register, the same construct class as Den norske kirke membership (Norway, BUILT) and folkekirken membership (Denmark, BUILT). A by-municipality or by-parish Svenska kyrkan product would follow that class: the mapped statistic is member count and members as a percentage of the parish or municipal population, with the SCB population-per-parish (or per-municipality) file as the denominator — the exact quantity the national PDF reports and the news maps compute. The product would be disclosed as single-denomination Lutheran membership, not a full affiliation frame, and the SST/MUCF served-population series would sit as national context for the non-Lutheran communities, never joined to the church register (different construct, different grain).

## Premise corrections (trust the record)

- **The parish or diocese route is no longer fully unverified.** Diocese membership totals are published on each diocese's "i siffror" page (Lund 782,107, thirteen dioceses). Parish and municipal membership is compiled by the church and supplied to media, with SCB parish population as the denominator; it is real and fine-grained, but no public machine-readable per-parish or per-municipality membership file was located. The queue's "unverified" is half right: the data exists at fine grain; the public machine-readable file does not.
- **"Browser work" overstates the national route and understates the subnational blocker.** The national series is a clean PDF text extract with no browser needed. The subnational route is blocked not by browser scraping but by the absence of a located per-unit membership file — the fix is a data release or an SCB commission, as the Finnish-background XLSX shows.
- **No modern census affiliation series is correct, and SCB holds no membership table.** SCB's statistical database has no religion area; church membership left official statistics at the 2000 separation. SCB supplies population per parish and the parish frame, not membership.
- **Boundaries are available, contrary to the README's open flag.** The README says church geographies need a separate boundary source; the church `Församlingskartor` API (parish/pastorat/diocese) and Lantmäteriet Distrikt (1999 parish frame, CC0) both supply it, alongside SCB municipality CC0.

## Licence positions (verbatim where quoted)

- **Church statistics page — unstated.** No licence, terms, or copyright-reuse statement was located on `svenskakyrkan.se/statistik`, on the research-unit page, or on the diocese pages (2026-07-12). This is the Finland `kirkontilastot.fi` position — a vacuum rather than an explicit reservation. It gates both the national PDF series and any subnational church product.
- **Church API portal — mostly CC BY 4.0, © Svenska kyrkan, per-API agreement.** Verbatim from the portal: "De flesta resurserna ... går nu under licensen CC BY 4.0"; "Varje API har sitt egna avtal som man måste godkänna." This covers the geography APIs (`Församlingskartor`), not the statistics PDFs.
- **SCB open geodata and population files — CC0.** "Vi använder licensen CC0 för dessa data ... utan krav på att ange källa" (digital boundaries page). SCB open data is CC0 generally.
- **Lantmäteriet open data — CC0.** "du får använda, sprida, göra om, modifiera och bygga vidare på Lantmäteriets öppna data", attribution encouraged not required.
- **SST / MUCF served-population tables — unstated.** No reuse terms located on the MUCF `trossamfund-antal-betjanade` page (2026-07-12).

## Blockers and held items

- **No public per-parish or per-municipality church-membership file (the ceiling for the subnational map).** The church compiles and distributes membership at these grains, but no public machine-readable download was located. The unblock is a data release, a media-file location, or an SCB commissioned cross-tab (the Finnish-background XLSX proves SCB can deliver membership by LKF parish code).
