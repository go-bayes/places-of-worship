# Suriname census-religion route probe

Verified 2026-07-12. PROBE ONLY — no build. **Verdict: HELD on the subnational-counts gate.** Suriname's Algemeen Bureau voor de Statistiek (ABS) publishes census religion openly, but only at the **national** level for the 2004 and 2012 censuses; a religion-by-district count table exists openly for **only three of the ten districts** (Marowijne, Brokopondo, Sipaliwini, in the Districtsresultaten Volume III presentation). The seven remaining districts — Paramaribo, Wanica, Nickerie, Coronie, Saramacca, Commewijne, Para — have no openly published religion-by-district table in any wave. A ten-district area-summary product therefore cannot be built from the published record: seven of ten districts would be null, and the fragment cannot reconcile to the published national margin. The two clean edges of the chain are the licence (site-wide Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, quoted verbatim below) and the boundary (geoBoundaries SUR ADM1, ten districts, Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0). The single blocker is the data grain: religion is published to the district only for three districts. The exact unblock is an ABS request for the count-valued religion-by-district table for 2012 (ten districts; and 2004 if held), or recovery of the full 2014 Districtsresultaten Volume I and II publications (as distinct from the selected-results presentations that are the only versions online).

## Premise corrections (the record refutes the brief and queue row)

The queue row 63 reads: `2004-2012 | district; resort may be possible | census affiliation | browser work | probe then build`. The published record corrects three of its premises.

The first premise the record refutes is the district grain. Religion is **not** published by district for the full ten-district frame in either 2004 or 2012. The 2012 national publication (Census 8 Volume 1) carries religion at the national level only — broad groups for 2004 and 2012 (Tabel H3) and detailed 2012 denomination (Tabel H4) — with no religion-by-district cross-tab. The only district-grain religion tables that are openly downloadable sit in the Districtsresultaten Volume III presentation and cover three districts. Resort (ressort) grain for religion does not appear anywhere: ABS published ethnicity down to ressort openly (census8etn.pdf, table-5s ressort-by-ethnic-group), but religion never below the national total except for the three Volume III districts.

The second premise the record refutes is the two-wave span. The 2004 religion figure survives openly only as a national broad-group column inside the 2012 publication's Tabel H3 (five groups: Christianity, Hinduism, Islam, Other+None, Unknown). No 2004 religion-by-district table and no 2004 detailed-denomination table was located openly. So even the national religion series is asymmetric across the pair: 2012 has nineteen-category denomination detail, 2004 has only the five broad groups.

The third correction concerns the 2024 census. The Ninth General Census law was adopted by the National Assembly on 4 October 2024, and some 2024 results are beginning to appear (the data-availability matrix lists 2024 employment, housing-material, and household-electricity entries). No 2024 religion result is published. The 2024 wave is not a route.

The 2012 national religion figures reconcile internally. Tabel H3 broad groups (Christendom 262,320; Hindoeisme 120,623; Islam 75,053; Andere+Geen 66,560; Onbekend 17,082) sum to the printed national total 541,638. The 2004 column (200,744 + 98,240 + 66,307 + 50,334 + 77,204) sums to 492,829. The 2012 detailed denomination table (Tabel H4, nineteen categories from Rooms Katholiek 117,261 down to Onbekend 17,082) sums to 541,638. These are the load-bearing national numbers; they are national-only and do not map to the district boundary.

The three openly published district religion tables (2012 Districtsresultaten Vol III, "Bevolking naar Godsdienst (denominatie) en geslacht") are count-valued with a percent column and a printed district total: Marowijne total 18,294; Brokopondo 15,909; Sipaliwini 37,065. The denomination frame **varies by district** — Marowijne prints the full frame (Rooms Katholiek, Volle Evangelie, E.B.G., Overig Christen, Hindoe Sanatan, Overig Hindoe incl. Aryah, Islam Soenniet, Overig Islam incl. Ahmadyah, Javanisme, Andere godsdienst/Jodendom/Winti/Jehova's Getuigen, Geen godsdienst, Onbekend), while Brokopondo and Sipaliwini collapse the Hindu, Islam, and "other" lines. Even a three-district product would carry a district-varying frame, and the three districts sum to roughly 71,000 of the national 541,638 — a fragment, not a subnational wave.

## Why a build is blocked

A ten-district area-summary product needs religion counts for all ten districts that reconcile to the published national total. The published record supplies religion counts for three districts only. The seven missing districts hold roughly 87 percent of the national population (Paramaribo and Wanica alone are the two largest). The build cannot proceed under the project's standing rules: RENDER THE RECORD forbids inventing, redistributing, or backcasting the missing district cells (for example, allocating the national denomination detail across districts by ethnicity would be a redistribution), and the exact-margin rule cannot be met when seven district columns are absent. A three-district fragment is not a district product; it neither covers the frame nor reconciles to the national margin.

## Dead ends searched (documented so the next probe does not repeat them)

- **census-statistics-2012 / censusstatistieken-2012 pages**: fully inventoried, including a raw-HTML grep for download-manager IDs and non-`.pdf` links. The only district-level census files are the three "Districtsresultaten" presentations (Vol I: Paramaribo, Wanica; Vol II: Nickerie, Coronie, Saramacca, Commewijne, Para; Vol III: Marowijne, Brokopondo, Sipaliwini). Only Vol III's presentation includes religion; Vol I and Vol II presentations omit religion entirely (confirmed by `godsdienst` grep on the pdftotext extractions).
- **Census 8 Volume 1 national publication**: religion is crossed with ethnicity (Tabel 7.1–7.3) and nationality (Tabel 11.1–11.3) at the national level, and population is crossed with district (Tabel 5, 10, 17), but religion is never crossed with district. No religion-by-district table anywhere in the national volume.
- **SurinameCensusInfo (DevInfo)**: the page describes a 2004/2012 DevInfo product but links no live online query tool; no REDATAM/DevInfo instance URL was found. Not a route.
- **Data-availability matrix (databeschikbaarheidsmatrix)**: no religion (godsdienst/geloofsovertuiging) entry for any census year; the only census entries are 2024 employment, housing-material, and electricity.
- **Social-cultural statistics, Statistical Papers (papers 1–3 pages and the downloads widget)**: Statistical Paper 5 (2007) is "Godsdienst/geloofsovertuiging en werkzamen/werklozen" — religion crossed with employment status, national, from the 2004 census; not religion by district. No statistical paper gives religion by district.
- **Mozaïek van het Surinaamse volk (versie 5)**: an ABS analytical monograph; discusses religion analytically (marriage patterns by religion, section 8) but carries no religion-by-district table.
- **UN Statistics Division WPHC mirror** (`unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic/sources/census/wphc/Suriname/`): the directory returns HTTP 403 (no listing); the individual `SUR-Census2012-vol2.pdf` (national education/employment volume) is reachable but is not a religion-by-district source.
- **Web searches** for a full ten-district religion table (2004 and 2012 variants): none located; secondary sources report only the national shares and qualitative district notes (Hindus concentrated in Nickerie/Wanica/Saramacca).

## Blockers and the exact unblock

- **Data grain (the one genuine gate)**: religion is published to the district for only three of ten districts. A ten-district product is not buildable from the open record, and no clean partial product exists (three districts, district-varying frame, no national reconciliation). This is the HELD.
- **Unblock**: an ABS reuse-and-data request for the count-valued **religion-by-district table for all ten districts, 2012** (and 2004 if held), or discovery of the full 2014 Districtsresultaten Volume I and II publications (the versions online are selected-results presentations that omit religion for seven districts). The ABS produced full per-district publications in March–May 2014 that repeated the national tables — including religion — for each district; those full publications, not the presentations, are the missing artefact. ABS contact for the social division is listed on the site (Telefoon 473737 #236). Under BUILD-THEN-ASK this is a data-availability gap, not a licence gap: the licence (CC BY 4.0) and the boundary (geoBoundaries SUR ADM1, ODbL 1.0) are both clean and byte-recorded here.
- **Recommended queue-row text**: `probed 2026-07-12, HELD: religion published nationally for 2004 (broad groups) and 2012 (national broad + detailed denomination), but religion-by-district counts are open for only 3 of 10 districts (Marowijne, Brokopondo, Sipaliwini, Districtsresultaten Vol III presentation); the 7 largest districts including Paramaribo and Wanica have no open religion-by-district table in any wave, so a 10-district product cannot reconcile. Licence CC BY 4.0 (verbatim) and boundary geoBoundaries SUR ADM1 (ODbL 1.0, 10 districts) are both clean. 2024 (9th census) adopted 2024-10-04, no religion result yet.
