## Build recipe

No build recipe for affiliation mapping. A future site-inventory track would need explicit risk review and source permissions before any public map.

## Risks and open questions

Religion mapping in Tajikistan carries high sensitivity because state regulation, mosque controls, minority communities, and security framing affect both data and public risk. Do not infer subnational composition from national estimates.

## Deep-history potential

Medium. The 1897 Russian Empire census, Soviet census materials, Tajikistan national archives, Committee on Religion records, mosque records, church records, and regional archives may support restricted historical research.
