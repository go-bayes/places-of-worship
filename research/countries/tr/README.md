## First visualisation

Do not build a religion-affiliation map from current public evidence.

## Build recipe

No build recipe for affiliation mapping. A mosque or registered-site map would require separate construct labels and sensitivity review.

## Risks and open questions

Religion data in Turkey are sensitive for Alevi, non-Muslim, and irreligious communities, and civil-status or mosque-administration records do not measure personal affiliation. Public products should avoid inferring affiliation from official mosque provision.

## Deep-history potential

High. Ottoman population registers, salnames, vakif records, Diyanet mosque data, Directorate General of Foundations archives, minority community archives, 1927 and later census volumes, and State Archives holdings can support historical site work.
