## Build recipe

No build recipe for affiliation mapping. A registered-organisation map would need a separate construct statement and risk review.

## Risks and open questions

Religion data in Uzbekistan are sensitive because state registration, minority communities, and security policy affect what can be safely published. National estimates and registered-organisation counts should not be disaggregated without official evidence.

## Deep-history potential

Medium. The 1897 Russian Empire census, Soviet census materials, Uzbekistan national archives, Committee on Religious Affairs records, mosque records, Orthodox diocesan records, and regional archives can support restricted historical research.
